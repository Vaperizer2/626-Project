Small model weights (MobileCLIP2-S3)
====================================

The "Small" model (exports/mobileclip2_s3) uses the image tower of Apple's MobileCLIP2-S3
(https://huggingface.co/apple/MobileCLIP2-S3, timm checkpoint fastvit_mci3.apple_mclip2_dfndr2b), released under the
Apple Machine Learning Research Model License ("apple-amlr"). That licence permits research and educational use and
restricts commercial use; read it before redistributing this bundle for any other purpose. The two linear heads on top
of it were fitted on the dataset described in DATASET.txt. SigLIP (Deployed, Fast, C) is Apache-2.0 and unaffected.
